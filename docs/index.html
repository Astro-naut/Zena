YouTube, minus whatever you don't want.

Most focus extensions hide everything or nothing. Zena gives every distraction its own switch — homepage recommendations, the sidebar, Shorts, comments, autoplay — so the feed you get is the one you chose.

One switch per distraction

Every hide-rule works on its own, so nothing you didn't ask for disappears. Keep the homepage clean but leave comments alone. Kill the sidebar but keep Shorts. It's your feed to edit, one toggle at a time.

01

Homepage recommendations

Hide the suggested-video feed on youtube.com. On by default.

02

Sidebar recommendations

Hide "Up next" suggestions beside a playing video. On by default.

03

End-screen suggestions

Remove the suggestion cards that cover a video in its last seconds.

04

Shorts

Hide Shorts shelves and the Shorts entry in the side nav.

05

Comments & live chat

Hide the comment section or the live chat panel on streams.

06

Explore, Trending & Autoplay

Tidy the left-hand guide and turn off YouTube's autoplay switch.

Free and open source

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox. Grab the code and install steps from GitHub.
